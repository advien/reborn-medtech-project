### How this project maps to my experience

Reborn directly reflects how I work with complex systems in practice:

- **From ML to system behavior**  
  In my production ML work, I focus on robustness, inference constraints, and predictable failure modes.  
  Reborn applies the same mindset to the physical world: ML is bounded, optional, and subordinate to system logic and safety.

- **Production thinking, not prototypes for demos**  
  I design systems around real constraints: noisy inputs, latency, partial observability, and human adaptation — not idealized data or benchmarks.

- **Clear interfaces and responsibility boundaries**  
  Similar to my MLOps and inference pipelines, Reborn separates concerns explicitly (sensing, QC, decision, safety), making the system debuggable and explainable.

- **Safety and trust over raw performance**  
  In both software and assistive systems, I prioritize predictable behavior and user trust over aggressive optimization.

- **Cross-domain communication**  
  This project demonstrates my ability to reason across ML, control logic, and human interaction — and to document decisions in a way that is understandable to engineers beyond my own specialization.
